    How the scores work

    • Every report should be cleaned within 48 hours. If it is not, it is escalated to a senior officer automatically.
    • Ward score = 60% for reports cleaned within 48 hours + 40% for reports cleaned at all. A ward needs at least 3 reports in the period to get a grade: A is 85 or more, B 70, C 50, D below 50.
    • "Proof of clean": the AI compares the after photo with the original report photo and checks the staff phone's location, so a cleaning cannot be faked with a photo of another street.
    • No names, phone numbers or exact addresses are shown on this page.
