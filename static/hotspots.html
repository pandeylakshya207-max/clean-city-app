How hotspots are found
  • Reports within 200 metres of each other are grouped. A place needs at least 3 reports to become a hotspot.
  • Recent reports count more: a report loses half its weight every 30 days.
  • A report that comes in after the spot was already cleaned counts double, because it shows people are dumping there again and again.
  • The expected next dumping date is the last cleaning plus the average time garbage took to come back. It is an estimate.
